# Realization: scope, coupling, production, ecosystem

When the scope is literally everything ([User intent](../intent/user-intent.md) §9), lowering generalizes to constructing a realization of a partially specified situation under an intent, a design scope, and available capabilities. The target is part of the problem; decomposition is a design variable; a realization includes its own coming-into-existence; standards are priced at ecosystem scale; the purpose is accountable realization. The situation object and the coupling model are proposals.

## The primary object (proposal)

Situation, intent, design scope, capabilities, realizations, evidence, continuity ([Schemas](schemas.md)). The operation: given these, construct a useful realization or a useful next step toward one. "Program" and "target" are roles within the task; a realization may stay partial so later decisions can use information unavailable now; tasks create tasks of other kinds, possibly cyclically (a software requirement produces a circuit problem, which produces a cooling problem, which invalidates the software partition).

| Task | Fixed | Open |
|---|---|---|
| chess move | position, rules, time | move, search strategy, opponent model |
| port an app to the web | required behavior, existing data | interaction design, client/server split, storage, algorithms |
| database | exact operations | indexes, replicas, consistency, placement, migration |
| game engine, video editor, CAD | required behavior, assets | layout, timing, hot reload, device mix, production capabilities |
| build an ASIC | operations and interfaces | microarchitecture, representation, scheduling, layout |
| mixed physical product | user requirements, volume | software, custom silicon, commodity parts, PCB, enclosure, assembly |
| HFT | latency contracts | decision policy, software/hardware partition |
| logistics, spacecraft control | commitments, models | schedules, controllers, monitoring, resource guarantees |
| factory | required production, site | machinery, layout, tooling, logistics, staffing, control software |
| building, city | existing infrastructure, authorized objectives | physical changes, scheduling, services, procurement, policy |

Degrees of freedom need structure: changing the implementation, the interface, the business process, and the requirement have different consequences and sources of authority. Lowering becomes compilation, synthesis, planning, control, inference, or *experimentation* (construct a measurement because the next decision depends on something unknown), each step stating which relation it claims ([Observers](observers.md)). Design scope records which freedoms are available and each commitment's scope, source, purpose, dependencies, and revision rule ([Commitment](commitment.md), [Schemas](schemas.md)); open is a compositional algebra of permitted transformations, including conditional permission and changes to the scope itself.

## Decomposition is a design variable

"The application", "the database", "the electronics", "the enclosure", "the process" need not be optimized independently: a different compression representation can eliminate a memory chip, which changes the PCB, power, cooling, enclosure, assembly, shipping. The question is whether expensive implementation work is compensating for a design choice elsewhere that one is allowed to change; monotone co-design is a formal starting point for a restricted class. Boundaries are stable because they are treaties letting independent parties work with limited mutual knowledge, and Conway's law is about authority rather than architecture, so the system *proposes* a boundary and adopts it only with the authority it rearranges (Q32; the economics of boundaries as standards are in [Commitment](commitment.md)). Where the domains already live: HFT is binding-time extremism (decisions compiled into an FPGA at the NIC); avionics is the obligation-and-evidence corner (assurance cases are evidence graphs); game engines are views plus data-oriented layout plus hot reload; factories and cities are digital twins and multi-party intent. The stack does not invent these corners; it lets them share tissue.

## What the universality is made of

Not domain solutions (a geometry kernel and an authorization engine share nothing in their theories) but connective tissue: identity, provenance, versioning, incremental dependency, search orchestration, evidence, transitions ([Correspondences](correspondences.md)). The skeptical reading is that a framework fitting everything says nothing; the defensible reading is that this tissue is what every domain reinvents badly and where most accidental engineering complexity lives. Model-based systems engineering was the previous attempt at one model of everything and struggles because its cross-domain relationships are authored documentation rather than derived and checked; this is a hypothesis to investigate, not a fact about MBSE (C53).

The tissue is infrastructure (C60), and its own bet is that it transfers: services built for one domain serve an alien one unchanged, with the per-instance consequences of a coupling between them derived from reusable domain models rather than wired by hand ([E5](../experiments/connective-tissue.md)). The project's other claims live in domain packages: lowering as search over declared laws beats fixed pipelines on a broad class; plural models and stratified controllers run in production without oscillating while their bookkeeping compiles away; a palette generator yields competitive silicon. Each has a named test ([Spine](spine.md), the bets). The tissue earns its place by making those cheaper to attempt and their evidence shareable.

**Coupling models (proposal).** The hardest shared problem may be the relationships *between* domains, today held in interface control documents and traceability matrices maintained by humans. A coupling ([Schemas](schemas.md)) records source and target concepts with units; whether the relation is exact, conservative, empirical, or heuristic; applicability; evidence; invalidation predicates. A software timing requirement constrains a component choice, which constrains power delivery and a PCB interface, which constrain geometry, cooling, enclosure, sourcing, assembly, and the feasible software schedule; a connector substitution can change geometry and signal behavior while its logical pin function is unchanged; a revised assumption should invalidate only the conclusions that depend on it and trigger the appropriate re-analyses. Composition may be cyclic and need fixed-point methods; partial models are acceptable if their limits are visible; a component model relating provided functionality, candidate implementations, and required resources composes into feasible alternatives and Pareto tradeoffs for a restricted class. Success means the domain packages supply the physical relationships, component meanings, and assumptions once, as models, and the particular cross-domain consequences for an instance are *derived from them and checked*; edits propagate and explanations survive; wiring each instance's consequences by hand, or a shared UI over manually synchronized files, does not count ([E5](../experiments/connective-tissue.md), Q31). Leads: MBSE, SysML, digital twins, interface control documents, traceability matrices, assurance cases, ISA-95/MES.

**Extensibility includes meaning.** A domain package contributes concepts and representations; laws, units, invariants, behavioral models; interpretations of incomplete descriptions; candidate generators and rewrites; solvers, simulators, evaluators; evidence checkers; cost models; ways to realize or observe things in the world; editing views. Domains interact through explicit relationships sharing the tissue; views across disciplines edit the same entities ([Store](store.md)). The extension interface must be strong enough for semantic interoperability and small enough not to force every domain into one solver, store, or per-operation protocol (Q01).

## A realization includes its production

A blueprint without a feasible manufacturing, transport, assembly, calibration, and maintenance process is incomplete; the physical realization package ([Schemas](schemas.md)) is derived from shared entities so its parts cannot slowly disagree. Resources (tools, materials, energy, machines, transport, inventory, expertise, time) are part of the problem; tooling can itself require a realization; a blueprint need not be producible with current capabilities, and acquiring them is another design problem; product and process are evaluated together (lower unit energy may lose on tooling cost at low volume; a standard connector may cost space and save assembly and replacement cost). The instance is not the model: measurements, calibration, and acceptance evidence connect them (the digital-twin relation), and a substitution invalidates some analyses while preserving others. Physical transitions preserve different obligations from code updates (occupancy, partly finished products, delivery commitments, environmental conditions, active processes), are not generally reversible or atomic, and are part of the realization rather than a footnote ([Change](change.md)).

**The hybrid-product case** (a sketch, not a chosen product). Inputs: required behavior, interaction, production quantity, power and size limits, available tools, desired lifecycle, permitted redesign. Candidates trade a custom datapath against commodity compute, change a representation to shrink memory, change cooling or packaging. Stress cases: a component becomes unavailable; a timing requirement tightens; the first manufactured instance differs from its model; a firmware update changes a resource assumption. The system exposes affected claims and possible changes rather than silently regenerating unrelated artifacts. Open: composing symbolic designs, continuous models, empirical tolerances, production schedules, authority, and physical observation without one solver or one truth status (Q30).

## The ecosystem objective

Optimizing one artifact in isolation makes custom-everything attractive. Across a thousand projects the objective is the cost of developing and sustaining a component ecosystem plus the sum of each project's lifecycle cost within it, including uncertain future projects. Standard components buy shared options (inventory, suppliers, know-how, evidence, tooling, replacement paths, compatibility); custom designs may win on operating cost and lose on effort and reuse. The pricing of standards and the choice of where to standardize are in [Commitment](commitment.md). Use multi-objective or conditional preferences where a weighted sum would hide disagreement. Open: network effects, uncertain future demand, supplier concentration, correlated failure, organizational knowledge, the cost of changing a standard, without claiming a perfect future distribution (Q35).

## Intent, authority, accountability

Intent is plural: exact constraints, measurable targets, preferences, examples, and unresolved disagreements coexist. The optimizer's objective is a model of intent, inspectable separately from the implementation; an AI-proposed specification is a model of intent, not the stakeholder's authorization. Authority is distributed; a proposed change is not authorized by its predicted benefit. Continuity includes occupied areas, in-progress products, accepted orders, and irreversible actions. Arrow and Goodhart apply with their actual assumptions ([Irreducibles](irreducibles.md)). With conflicting objectives the stack represents, surfaces, or defers to a named authority; the interface for an unresolved disagreement is open (Q33). A business process is a program whose cells include people, with contracts for capability, latency, and error rate, and whose authority is real authority; a reorganization is a boundary operation with authority transfer ([Change](change.md)); regulation enters the permitted column as policy, and a building code is a checker over geometry.

Situation, intent, scope, capabilities, realization, evidence, continuity is a planning domain plus a design problem plus an epistemic state. "Compile this situation toward that intent under this authority" is what an agent does, and agents will do a growing share of realizing, so the substrate's most valuable property is that every realization carries its intent, authority, evidence, and transition obligations and can be inspected, contested, and undone. **Accountable realization** is the purpose. Compiler-as-library and agent-as-participant are the same object once lowering means "construct a realization with explicit evidence"; agents' outputs are candidates for the checkers ([Justification](justification.md), Q34). Open: Q31–Q36.
