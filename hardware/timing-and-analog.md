# Timing, asynchrony, analog, and not-bits

Daniel asked to think all the way down to asynchronous computation and movement not measured in bytes or bits, with no clock that makes sense. The governing facts: monotone computation needs no arbitration and non-monotone computation needs arbitration with unbounded worst case ([Irreducibles](../design/irreducibles.md)); analog must live inside restoring boundaries; "not bits" is always a point on the energy-versus-precision curve; the freedom worth having is no *architectural* global clock, with timing disciplines composed per region.

## Clockless

A clock is a consensus protocol on the value of time, broadcast on a wire tree, with margin burned on the slowest path. Delay-insensitive circuits (Martin's CHP→QDI synthesis at Caltech; Sutherland's micropipelines; Fulcrum, Achronix) replace it with local handshakes; GALS is what large chips already are in practice. Async lost on tooling, not physics: synthesis, static timing analysis, and test are built around the clock, and the clock is itself a treaty letting designers compose blocks without reading each other's timing.

"No clock anywhere" is an additional restriction whose benefit must be shown (C24): even QDI relies on isochronic-fork timing assumptions, and local handshakes cost area and energy. Timing disciplines (synchronous islands, self-timed, GALS, event-driven, continuous-time) compose per region; the interface contracts between them, including metastability budgets, are open (Q28). Three qualifications. A time-division slot table is a shared time base, so a slotted fabric has not removed arbitration, only moved it to schedule time; the design abolishes the architectural global clock and makes a time base a leasable cell with stated precision ([Participation](../design/participation.md)). Pausible clocks have had little industrial uptake; the shipped form of mixed timing is dual-clock FIFOs with a budgeted mean time between synchronizer failures, and any asynchronous input into a real-time region carries that probability ([Deadlines](../design/deadlines.md)). Which discipline a cell should use (synchronous, bundled-data, quasi-delay-insensitive) is decided by simulating one cell of identical function in all three through the process kit, energy against voltage at matched throughput, not by tooling comfort ([Fabrication](fabrication.md)). Asynchronous-first designs trade variation and temperature tolerance, free idleness, and no clock-tree power against roughly doubled area for dual-rail logic, weaker dense throughput, and unsolved test at scale (figures to verify). Timing contracts cover causal order, throughput, buffer behavior, latency envelopes, deadlines, sampling windows, and uncertainty in physical time. Static schedules encode order implicitly; dynamic work needs explicit timestamps or credits; neither is universally better. Fully asynchronous abstract models still need resource and progress arguments in finite implementations. The dataflow and spatial lineage is indexed in [Systems](../evidence/systems.md), hardware.

## Not bits

Hardware is already analog under a digital facade at every boundary: a NAND cell stores 3–4 bits as 8–16 voltage levels; a DRAM cell is a capacitor read by a sense amplifier; every bus is a transmission line. The digital abstraction (Shannon 1937; von Neumann 1956) is valuable for one reason: **restoration**. Gates regenerate signals, so error does not accumulate and composition is unbounded. Analog does not restore, so it works only for shallow, error-tolerant stages (one matrix multiply in a memristor crossbar, one MZI mesh in a photonic chip) with digital boundaries between. Hence **typed analog**: a restoring boundary is a consistency envelope for signal integrity, and the type system is where the envelope and error model are declared. The boundary restores the digital *interpretation*; it does not recover numerical information lost in the analog stage to noise, clipping, aliasing, or quantization (C29).

Shannon gives the exchange rate: effective bits ≈ log₂(1+SNR), so "not bits" is a point on the energy-versus-precision curve and never an escape from it. Already there informally: FP8, FP4, MX block formats, posits, stochastic rounding. Research types compose reliability through a program (EnerJ 2011, Rely 2013; [Justification](../design/justification.md)). Stochastic computing (value as bit-stream probability; multiply is one AND gate), spiking and temporal codes (information in event timing), p-bits and thermodynamic computing (noise as the resource) are alternative encodings; the brain is the existence proof for the bundle, though efficiency comparisons need care. Seen from the generator, variation is the input: it emits a distribution of instances, the self-description is the per-instance realization, and "exact digital" is the special case in which margin has narrowed the distribution. That dissolves the analog/digital split into one tolerance axis and makes probabilistic hardware the general case ([Quantities](../design/quantities.md)).

## Natively probabilistic hardware

p-bit arrays, Ising machines, thermodynamic samplers, memristor crossbars with a few effective bits: hardware whose primitive is "sample from this distribution" or "compute approximately with this noise". If the program's semantics include `sample` and `approximately`, they lower to such cells the way a matrix multiply lowers to a tensor core, with the inference strategy as the mapping ([Justification](../design/justification.md) for the three sources of probability and the error-budget discipline). Stochastic rounding in training is the small existing instance. Engineering maturity of these substrates is a lead, not an established fact. Error contracts for analog and stochastic cells must compose under correlated faults (Q29). Analog, stochastic, and conversion cells enter the generator palette as contract-conforming cells ([Chipset family](chipset-family.md)).
