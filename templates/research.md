# Research note

## Reference and question
Title, authors, date or version, primary link, access date, and the question that prompted reading.

## Actual result
The supported claim, its assumptions, and its model. Do not transfer a theorem by analogy.

## Evidence and limits
Theorem, experiment, or documentation; sections checked; workload; limitations; unresolved details.

## Relevance
What can be reused; what has different semantics; links to claims, open questions, and experiments.

## Next step
One concrete comparison, reproduction, or design consequence. A named reference alone is not verification.
