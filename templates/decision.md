# Decision record

## Status and authority
Proposed / accepted / superseded. Who authorized it, and when. Proposals stay proposed without Daniel's direction.

## Problem and scope
Which decision is being made, what stays open, which requirements apply.

## Alternatives
Behavior, evidence, costs, reversibility, retained options.

## Choice and rationale
The choice and its strongest reason; assumptions that would change it.

## Consequences and revisit trigger
What gets harder or impossible; what evidence invalidates the choice; links to evidence and superseded decisions.
