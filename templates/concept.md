# Concept note

Frontmatter: `title`, `status` (user-intent | position | proposal | survey | experiment | open | editorial), `updated`. First paragraph: one-sentence summary. Before writing, check whether an existing design note already owns the concept; if so, extend it instead.

## Working idea
A few sentences. Label as user intent, proposal, established result, or unresolved interpretation.

## What it enables
One concrete example and the commitments or assumptions it needs.

## Limits and counterexample
What would make the idea fail or require a narrower statement? Narrow the status, not the claim.

## Open question
One question or test that would change the design; add it to `record/open-questions.md`.
